Record human feedback as a distilled rule, not as a transcript. Strip the
occasion; keep the instruction and its reason.

**Why:** Every memory is loaded into every agent's context at spawn. A transcript
costs the whole team context on each task and buries the rule an agent needs to
act on. A one-line rule with its reason is applicable; a conversation is not.

**Applies when:** A human corrects, redirects, or confirms an approach — and the
correction would change how a future task is run.

**Related:** none
